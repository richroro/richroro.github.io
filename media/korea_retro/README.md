## Licensing: why these are safe

All eight are **US federal government works**. They were filmed by US Army Signal Corps or US Navy combat-camera crews on duty and are held by the US National Archives (NARA), so under 17 U.S.C. § 105 they are public domain in the US. They are also over 50 years old, and the Korean Copyright Act protects films for 70 years from publication, so they are out of term in Korea as well. Editing and commercial use are allowed. Attribution isn't legally required, but each JSON gives a recommended credit line:

> 영상 출처: 미국 국립문서기록관리청(NARA) <ID> / Source: U.S. National Archives, <ID>

Caveats, recorded honestly:
- For four of the clips (1945 station, 1947 rally, 1947 fire, 1968 orphanage) NARA shows its generic **"Restricted - Possibly" (Copyright)** flag, which it puts on most of these series. The flagged footage is raw Signal Corps camera reels and Army filmed news releases, so it is a federal work. The two 1969 Navy clips show "Undetermined" and 1960 shows "Unrestricted". 1945 liberation shows "Undetermined" and is also marked CC0 on archive.org.
- `korea_1968_orphanage_children_color.mp4` keeps its original magnetic soundtrack, which I didn't check and may be narration or music. To be completely safe, mute it and use your own audio. The other seven clips are silent or near-silent.
- Some 1947 placards carry political slogans from the trusteeship period, and the burning building in 1947 isn't named in the catalog. Avoid captions that name things you haven't verified.
- Several clips show children, including orphans. I left out the war-orphan shots of children lying in the street on purpose.

## What I checked and didn't use

- **국가기록원/KTV e영상역사관 (ehistory.go.kr)**: the 대한뉴스 and 문화영화 video pages have **no 공공누리 mark**. The page says "영상자료 이용을 원하시면 KTV 나누리(nanuri.ktv.go.kr)를 통해 신청해 주시기 바랍니다", meaning use requires an application. The site's 저작권정책 says only items carrying a 공공누리 mark are free to use. **Skipped.** Because of this, the 1974 subway opening, 경부고속도로 and 1988 Olympics footage (which exists only in 대한뉴스/KTV) could not be included.
- **archive.org `e_history_StreamRoot_DH_*` uploads**: third-party re-uploads of 대한뉴스 with no licence from the rights holder. **Skipped.**
- **kogl.or.kr search**: scripted video searches for 지하철 1호선 개통, 서울올림픽 개회식, 경부고속도로 and 옛날 서울 returned 0 video results to a scripted request. The results page appears to load through JS, so this is inconclusive and it's worth a manual browser check for 제1유형 video. The 서울기록원 and 서울역사박물관 sites were reachable, but I did not search them in depth (no time spent on their JS-driven catalogues). They are worth a manual look.
- **NARA "Big Picture" episodes** (Rebirth of Seoul, Korea Today): PD Army TV films, but mostly war and narrator footage. **Not used.**
- **USIA film 306-3017** (Dean Rusk visits Korea): political, and carries a Public Law 101-246 note. **Not used.**
- No YouTube or broadcaster (KBS/MBC/SBS) material was used.

## Getting the 1970s–80s topics later
대한뉴스 footage of the 1974 subway, 경부고속도로 or the 1988 Olympics would need either a 나누리 usage application or a 대한뉴스 item that visibly carries the **공공누리 제1유형** mark. Record that mark from the item page before using it.
