## Why these are PD

- **US:** works published in 1930 or earlier are PD (the 1930 term ran out on 1 Jan 2026). The 1941 Superman shorts lapsed because National never renewed them. Private Snafu was made for the War Department and released without copyright.
- **Korea:** Copyright Act Art. 42 protects films for 70 years from publication, and before July 2013 it was 50 years with no revival. Every film here is from 1955 or earlier, so all are PD in Korea.

## Caveats (read before branding)

- **Trademarks:** Mickey Mouse (Disney), Superman and the S-shield (DC), Betty Boop (Fleischer Studios/King Features) and Felix the Cat (DreamWorks/NBCU) are live trademarks. You can use the footage in videos. Don't put these characters in a channel name, logo, thumbnail branding or merchandise, and don't imply any endorsement.
- **Superman underlying work:** the comic (Action Comics #1, 1938) is still under copyright, in the US until 2034. Russell v. Price leaves some theoretical risk even for a PD film. In practice these shorts circulate freely, but this is the least clean item. Only Mickey's 1928 design and Betty's 1930 design are PD; their later designs are not.
- **Private Snafu** contains swastikas, Hitler and wartime caricature. Present it as history, because YouTube may limit ads on it.
- The 1950s U.M.&M. reissue title card on Dizzy Dishes and the Filmcollectief restoration credit on Felix are outside the cuts.
