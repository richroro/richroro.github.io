## Notes
- **Silent clips:** `black_hole_plunge.mp4` and `sdo_solar_flare.mp4` have their audio replaced by a silent track because the original soundtracks use production music that is not public domain. Add your own music or narration.
- **NASA media guidelines:** don't imply NASA endorsement, and don't use the NASA logo/insignia as channel branding. Credit line suggestion: "영상: NASA".
- **Not included:** no ISS night, aurora or Korea-at-night footage turned up in the NASA image library, so `iss_earth_views` is daytime. The Webb launch-and-deploy animation was skipped because it shows Arianespace/ESA branding, and the Webb first-images highlights were skipped because they carry NASA/ESA/CSA/STScI credits. Crew Dragon DM-2 docking (`jsc2020m000166_DM-2_Docking_200531`) and SVS 13326 (a 15 s black-hole accretion-disk loop) are good alternates that weren't cut.
